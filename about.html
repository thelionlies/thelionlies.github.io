About

Ivan Yuri "Lion" De Leon writes under two names. As Lion, he researches AI safety and Filipino NLP. As Láyon, he publishes poetry in Filipino. He is one person with one preoccupation: what happens when the systems we build become more capable than the people who built them, and what that means for what it means to be human.

He is a Master's student in Data Science at Ateneo de Manila University and a graduate of Applied Mathematics, Summa Cum Laude, with a minor in Economics. He received the Mulry Award for Literary Excellence and the Ateneo College Awards for the Arts in Poetry (both 2025), and the National Youth Achievement in Literary Arts from the National Committee on Literary Arts. He has done research at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST) in Japan and interned at Ayala Corporation and BPI. He is an AI Safety participant at TARA and AI Safety Diliman, and a graduate assistant for the Mathematics and Environmental Science Departments at Ateneo.

His research focuses on AI and language — Filipino NLP, large language models, and human–AI interaction. He is conducting benchmarking studies to support the development and evaluation of Filipino in NLP systems. During his undergraduate years, he served as Managing Editor for Finance at HEIGHTS Ateneo, the university's official literary publication.

His poetry appears in HEIGHTS, TLDTD, Palihang LIRA anthologies, and Bente-Bente Zine. He was a winner of the Pambansang Hamon sa Pagtula in 2023 and 2024. He is a member of Linangan sa Imahen, Retorika, at Anyo (LIRA), the oldest organization of poets who write in the Filipino language.

The two names exist because the communities are different. The concerns are the same. He keeps building anyway. He writes about it in Filipino because that language holds the tension best.
