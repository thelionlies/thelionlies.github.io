About

Láyon is a poet who writes in Filipino. They write in the small hours they steal from their busy world, about the things they cannot stop thinking about: the posthuman, the artificial in AI, the paranoia of how the artificial may soon (or will) cause the demise of the posthuman, and what can be done to prevent it.

They took their Master's in Data Science at Ateneo de Manila University and graduated Summa Cum Laude in Applied Mathematics with a minor in Economics. They received the Mulry Award for Literary Excellence, the Ateneo College Awards for the Arts in Poetry, and the National Youth Achievement in Literary Arts from the National Committee on Literary Arts (all in 2025).

Their poetry appears in HEIGHTS, TLDTD, Palihang LIRA anthologies, and Bente-Bente Zine. They were a winner of the Pambansang Hamon sa Pagtula in 2023 and 2024. They are a member of Linangan sa Imahen, Retorika, at Anyo (LIRA), the oldest organization of poets who write in the Filipino language. During their undergraduate years, they served as Managing Editor for Finance at HEIGHTS Ateneo, the university's official literary publication.

Some call their work speculative. They prefer to think of it as philosophical, the natural output of a skeptic who distrusts easy answers and grand narratives, whether about the benefits of technology or the supernatural. Some call it experimental. They would say they are only writing honestly about a life lived close to technology, where things change faster than language can follow. Their poems are, in the end, poems of contradiction: analytical and emotional, scientific and lyrical, built from logic and reaching past it.

Akala ni Láyon matematika ang mapapangasawa niya. Ngunit nang makilala ang pagtula, hindi na mabuo-buo ang kanyang isip. Hanggang ngayon, hinahanap niya pa rin ang sariling layon.
