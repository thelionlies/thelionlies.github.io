Selected Series

Ang Pagtupad ng mga Robot sa Tatlong Batas ng Robotics — Three poems on Asimov's laws of robotics, written for and about machines. Published in Heights 70(2), 2023; reprinted in Metro Meka Mega, Bente-Bente Zine, 2024.

René — A collection of digitally manipulated self-portraits parodying works by Magritte, each paired with a poem meditating on the act of editing. Published in Heights 71(1), 2023.

Ang Hindi Sinasabi ng mga Makina Hinggil sa Hindi Natin Maunawaan — Three poems on singularity: initial, gravitational, technological. Metro Meka Mega, Bente-Bente Zine, 2024.

Mga Talâ Mula sa Klase ng Etika — Three satirical poems written from the vantage of philosophical ethics. Metro Meka Mega, Bente-Bente Zine, 2024.

Ilang Paraan ng Pagkagunaw — Glacier, Sinkhole, Missile. Metro Meka Mega, Bente-Bente Zine, 2024.

21st Century Maligno — Three poems on Filipino mythological creatures reimagined for the digital age. Kalat: Palihang LIRA Batch 37, 2022; reprinted in Metro Meka Mega.
