Research Projects

  • TanawPamana — A multimodal AI benchmark for recognizing Philippine cultural heritage sites, developed during a research internship at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST). Ongoing.
  • Evaluating LLM Stance-Taking on Philippine Current Affairs: A Case Study on the Impeachment of Sara Duterte — Developed a framework for evaluating LLM knowledge on time-sensitive topics such as current affairs. Final project for AI Safety Diliman's AI Governance Accelerator, in partnership with SIGLA Research Center.
  • TDR: LSTM-Based Autoencoders — Tagalog Diacritic Restoration using LSTM-based autoencoders on homographs, with evaluation on text from Virgilio Almario's Sari-Samot blogs.
  • TDR: Transformer-Based Models — Transformer-based Tagalog Diacritic Restoration with corpus expansion, linguistic analysis, and model experimentation.

Software Projects

  • BEA: Blue Eagle Assistant — AI-powered university chatbot built with Streamlit. Answers Ateneo student questions using handbook references and policy context.
  • FIES Viewer — Django web application for exploring household economic data from the Family Income and Expenditure Survey in CALABARZON, Philippines.
  • SIMS: Social Impact Mapping Simulation — Java two-party election simulation based on Social Impact Theory (Nowak, Szamrej & Latané, 1990), with GUI and dynamic social influence modeling.
