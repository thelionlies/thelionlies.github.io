Summary

I have worked on data science, machine learning, and AI projects across industry and research. As a Data Analytics and AI Intern at Ayala Corporation, one of the Philippines' largest and oldest conglomerates, I led a cross-functional initiative with Ayala and AC Mobility, its mobility subsidiary, to develop predictive models identifying key drivers of vehicle sales, informing promotion-effectiveness metrics and improving sales forecasting. I also analyzed workforce attrition and leave patterns for over 200 employees, built a real-time HR analytics dashboard that automated monthly reporting, modeled user behavior for more than 5,000 mobility app users using Markov chains to guide product development, and supported large-scale data engineering, preprocessing, and quality assurance pipelines. Previously, as a Data Science Intern at the Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI), the oldest bank in the Philippines and one of its largest financial institutions, I completed four end-to-end data science projects involving financial market data scraping, recommendation systems, customer segmentation, and explainable AI for credit risk modeling. Beyond industry, I conducted AI evaluation research as an intern at the Human–AI Interaction Lab of the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), where I developed a multimodal benchmark for vision-language models and built an LLM-as-a-judge evaluation pipeline. I am also pursuing research in AI safety, LLM evaluation, and Filipino NLP, with hands-on experience in transformers, mechanistic interpretability, reinforcement learning, and alignment through the TARA Program and ARENA's technical AI safety curriculum.
